Photosensitivity warning

A very small number of people may have a seizure when they see certain kinds of light, such as flashing lights or patterns, even if they have never had one before. One Billion Stars avoids flashing and strobing, but it does have bright glows, bursts of particles and fast movement.

If you feel dizzy, have blurred vision, eye or face twitching, or feel unwell in any way, stop playing straight away and rest. Effects can be turned down or off in Settings, under Picture.

Challenge

Linear rules, with these changes:

  • Sectors can’t be bought. The next one opens only when every star in your sectors is collected.
  • No helpers. The Fleet Hangar never appears, so every star is collected by you.
  • Half-strength boosts. Stellar Caches and discoveries give 50% less than in Linear.
  • Small steps. Upgrades come in many small levels instead of a few big ones, so there is always one within reach.
  • No skin effects. Cursor skins keep their look, but the Legendary and Mythic effects are off.

    Cloud backup
