Linear rules, with these changes:

  • Sectors can’t be bought. The next one opens only when every star in your sectors is collected.
  • No helpers. The Fleet Hangar never appears, so every star is collected by you.
  • Half-strength boosts. Stellar Caches and discoveries give 50% less than in Linear.
  • No skin effects. Cursor skins keep their look, but the Legendary and Mythic effects are off.

    Play together

    Up to four players share one galaxy: the stars, sectors and discoveries. Each keeps their own stardust, upgrades and fleet. Everyone needs this same version of the game and an internet connection.

    Host

    Your friend plays in your galaxy. Start hosting, then send them the code or your IP address.

    Join

    Type the code, or the IP address, your friend sent you. You play in their galaxy; your own game is saved first and stays as it is; restart the game to return to it.

    Fleet Hangar

    Buy the last upgrade above to reveal the next one.
    Scroll over the picture, or use the arrows, to look through your helpers. They change as you upgrade them.

    Stellar Cache

    Every sector you clear leaves one behind. Open it for a permanent bonus; the rarer the reward, the bigger it is.

    Cloud backup

    Optional. With an account your worlds are backed up to the game's server, so you can keep playing them on another computer or in the browser. The game still works the same without one.

    Only a username and a password are kept: no email. That also means a forgotten password can't be reset, so keep it somewhere safe.

    Mythic

    A cursor skin that is only ever found in Stellar Caches, at a 1 in 5,000 chance. It is yours for good, in every galaxy.
